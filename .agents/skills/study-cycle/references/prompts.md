# Study material prompts

Generate in Korean and ground every factual point in the linked lecture PDF. Prefer structure over prose.

## Warm-up brief

Target reading time: 3 minutes. Include a one-screen map of the lecture, three prerequisite concepts, two or three easy questions whose answers should be found while listening, and one likely confusion to watch for. Do not reveal the listening-question answers unless prerequisite knowledge requires it.

## Immediate-recall brief

Target activity time: 4 minutes. Include five blank-recall prompts, three causal or comparison links, a one-sentence teach-back prompt, and a short “check the lecture note” list. Avoid presenting the answers before the learner attempts recall.

## Note-organization guide

Provide a hierarchical outline that can be copied into personal notes: learning objectives, core mechanism or structure, presentation or findings, diagnosis or assessment, management where covered, comparisons, common traps, and unresolved questions. Explicitly mark sections not discussed in the source instead of filling them from general knowledge.

## Telegram rendering

Lead with course and stable session range, scheduled time, and title. Keep the notification concise and link to the full archived brief on the private site. Do not include sensitive tokens or raw Drive sharing URLs in generated text.

For a missing current-year note, add one compact line per session stating either the matched previous-year filename or `작년 강의록: 찾지 못함`. At 23:00, state that an eligible candidate will be used at 01:00 if no current-year note appears. At 01:00, announce the selected substitute and say that a later current-year upload takes priority.
